Class Imbalance Evaluation Methodology:
Financial fraud represents only 0.172% of all transactions. Relying solely on raw accuracy is misleading, as a trivial dummy model predicting 100% legitimate achieves 99.83% accuracy while failing to detect any fraud. In our industry-standard evaluation, we prioritize Recall (Fraud Detection Rate), Precision, F1-Score, and PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve).
